A top-down 2.5D open-world crime game for the [make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

**Sunset City, golden hour.** You are Rico Vale, getaway driver. Your fixer Mara Quinn
has six jobs for you: meet the crew, steal the lime Sunset GT, run the boulevard, lose the
heat, pull a bank heist, and make the golden getaway by boat. The higher your wanted level,
the longer the shadows grow and the hotter the city gets — **Golden Hour Heat**.

## What is in the game

- A clear open city to explore on foot and by car: downtown towers, Market Row, Palm Court
  houses, Sunset Park, a boardwalk beach and a marina — all on one readable grid with
  Sunset Boulevard as the main artery.
- Traffic, pedestrians, and police who chase you (plus a helicopter at 3+ stars).
- Cars to steal and drive — the lime Sunset GT, traffic cars, marked SPD cruisers.
- Wanted stars, combat (fists + pistol), 6 story missions, money + rep progression.
- HUD with minimap, mission panel, health, speed and weapon; start screen; save in the browser.
- Synthesized sound (engine, sirens, gunshots, jingles) — no audio files.
- Smooth Canvas 2D rendering: clean solid colours, crisp detail, 60 fps target.

## Play

Serve it locally and open http://localhost:8080:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Controls: **WASD / arrows** move & drive · **F** enter / exit car · **Click** shoot ·
**Space** punch · **1 / 2** fists / pistol · **Shift** run · **P** pause · **M** mute ·
**Wheel** zoom.
